Privacy Policy

Privacy-first beta capture.

HTML to Figma Bridge captures page structure only when you explicitly click capture in the browser extension. Captures may include page URLs, visible text, layout metadata, computed styles, image references, and inline SVG needed to create editable Figma layers.

What we collect

The tool receives capture packages generated by the browser extension and stores them temporarily on the backend so the Figma plugin can import them. We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment details, cookies, browser storage, or hidden form values.

Retention

Captures are temporary. The backend supports automatic retention cleanup through the CAPTURE_RETENTION_HOURS environment variable and a delete endpoint for individual captures.

Training and sharing

Captures are not used to train AI models by default. Do not submit captures containing private customer data, confidential dashboards, passwords, or regulated information unless you have permission.
