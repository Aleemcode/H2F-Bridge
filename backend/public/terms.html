Beta Terms

Use the beta responsibly.

HTML to Figma Bridge is an early beta tool for converting user-triggered webpage captures into editable Figma layers. Output fidelity is improving and should be reviewed before production design use.

Acceptable use

Only capture pages you are allowed to inspect and transform. Do not capture confidential, regulated, personal, or customer data without permission. Do not use the tool to copy designs in a way that violates another party's rights.

Beta limitations

The importer is not guaranteed to be pixel-perfect. Fonts, images, SVGs, advanced CSS, and responsive variants may require manual cleanup in Figma.
